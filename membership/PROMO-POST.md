# TCSO Membership Launch Post

## English

!!! TCSO MEMBERSHIP IS OPEN !!!

TCSO is welcoming members for its first membership intake. Connect with Myanmar communities around the world and stand together for democracy, dignity and a federal future for Myanmar. Learn, share opportunities and grow together.

**Who can apply?** Anyone currently living outside Myanmar. There is no age limit. You may also volunteer to be considered as a country coordinator. There may be no specific duties at first, but coordinators should be willing to help if a need comes up later.

### Free Member — US$0 TCSO membership fee

- Google AI Plus — Gemini AI access + 2 TB Google Drive storage (12 months)
- Super Duolingo (12 months)
- Apple Music Individual (6 months)
- Community, education and career resources

**TCSO membership fee: US$0.** Optional U.S. product-price comparison: about US$275.81* (this is not a TCSO charge).

### Paid Supporter Member — US$30 one-time community membership fee

Includes everything in Free, plus:

- LinkedIn Premium Career (12 months; U.S. list-price benchmark: US$239.88)
- Eligible LinkedIn Premium partner offers shown in the member’s account may include YouTube Premium Lite (3 months), Lovable (1 year + 300 one-time credits), Granola AI (6 months), Headway Premium (4 months), Headspace (4 months), Notion Business (3 months) and NordVPN Basic (3 months).

**TCSO membership fee: US$30, one time only.** The estimated U.S. retail comparison for the main listed subscriptions is about US$515.69* (not a TCSO charge). Eligible partner offers may provide additional trial access; they are not included in that comparison.


**Payment options:** Request the final amount in USD, SGD, THB, JPY or EUR, or ask TCSO which cryptocurrency and payment routes are currently supported. TCSO will confirm the one-time converted amount, exchange rate and payment instructions before you decide to pay. No payment is due with the application. Never send money or crypto to an unverified address.

**Apply:**

- Free membership: https://tcso.world/membership/?plan=free
- US$30 membership: https://tcso.world/membership/?plan=paid

Some partner offers require an active paid LinkedIn Premium account and can expire, so TCSO will share available partner offer instructions during the 12-month Premium period. Eligibility and terms vary.

The application page prepares a bilingual message. Review it, copy it, open TCSO Messenger, paste the message and tap Send to submit your application.

*Retail values use U.S. list-price comparisons: Google AI Plus 12 months at US$9.99/month, Super Duolingo 12 months at US$83.99, Apple Music Individual 6 months at US$11.99/month, and LinkedIn Premium Career 12 months at US$239.88/year. Prices and product plans vary by country. These are illustrative retail-equivalent values, not TCSO’s actual purchase cost or guaranteed cash savings. Partner offers depend on LinkedIn account eligibility, region and current terms; offer values are excluded from the totals. See full comparison and terms at https://tcso.world/membership/.*

*Bridging borders. Building futures. Together.*
