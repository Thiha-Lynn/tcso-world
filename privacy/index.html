Privacy & data deletion

Updated 8 November 2026. TCSO uses information you provide to verify your email, review membership applications and answer questions.

Registration

We collect your preferred name, country, email, selected plan and optional preferences. Brevo delivers verification emails. Codes expire in 10 minutes; verification records are removed within 48 hours. Application records are held privately on our existing AWS server for up to 90 days. No payment is collected through the form.

Messenger assistance

Meta operates Messenger and processes your messages under its policies. Our automated assistant receives messages sent to the TCSO Page. A welcome notice identifies our automated assistant. Questions are answered directly using DeepSeek; no activation keyword is needed. Tap Talk to team or type HUMAN or STOP to pause generated replies for staff follow-up. Tap Resume help to restart. We attempt to remove email addresses and long numbers before forwarding text; please do not send sensitive personal details, passwords, OTPs, payment information, identity documents or political views. AI can make mistakes and cannot approve applications, verify payments or promise activation. Staff make those decisions.

For follow-up questions, the assistant stores up to six recent question-and-answer exchanges per conversation for up to 24 hours and sends this context to DeepSeek. Basic email, long-number and application-reference redaction is applied, but it does not remove every kind of personal information. Type RESET or FORGET to clear this assistant memory. HUMAN handoff also clears it; messages sent while paused are not added to assistant memory. Clearing assistant memory does not erase the Meta inbox or provider records. The assistant keeps queued message text only until processing finishes, and removes queue records after two days. Your Page-scoped identifier and AI/human preference are kept for up to 30 days after changing that preference. Staff may still see messages in the Meta Page inbox; Meta and DeepSeek have their own retention policies. This service does not sell your information.

Request access or deletion

Message TCSO on Messenger with HUMAN and then request data access or deletion. Include your application reference if available. Staff will confirm the request relates to your information before deleting it. Please do not send identity documents. Deletion from TCSO's systems does not automatically delete Meta or email-provider copies.

Contact

Contact the TCSO team. The register@tcso.world address is an automated outgoing sender.
